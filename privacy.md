## Short version

Helmer collects **no personal data**. There are no user accounts, no analytics,
no tracking, no advertising and no third-party SDKs. Your position, speed and
anchor-watch data never leave your device.

## Who is responsible

Michael Beutler, Switzerland — an individual developer, not a company.

Questions about this policy: [support@iperka.com](mailto:support@iperka.com)

## Location data

Helmer asks for location access so it can show your position on the chart, your
GPS speed, the 300 m and 150 m shore zones of Art. 53 of the Swiss Inland
Navigation Ordinance (SR 747.201.1) around your boat, and — if you arm the
anchor watch — how far your boat has drifted.

- Location is read from your device's GPS and **processed entirely on the
  device**.
- It is **never transmitted** to me, to a server, or to any third party.
- If you arm the anchor watch, Helmer stores the anchor position, the alarm
  radius and the drift trail in the app's local storage on your device, so the
  alarm survives an app relaunch or a phone restart. Disarming the anchor watch
  clears it.
- Background ("Always") location access is used **only** for the anchor-drift
  alarm, so it can keep watching your boat while your phone is locked. You can
  decline it and still use every other feature.

You can revoke location access at any time in **Settings → Privacy & Security →
Location Services → Helmer**.

## Settings stored on your device

Preferences such as your speed unit (km/h or knots), cruise speed and routing
options are stored locally on your device. They contain no personal
information and are not synced to any cloud service.

## Deleting your data

All data Helmer holds is local to your device. Deleting the app removes it
completely. There is nothing for me to delete on my side, because I never
receive anything.

## Network connections

Helmer has exactly **one** network connection:

**Wind measurements from MeteoSwiss.** To show current wind and storm warnings,
Helmer downloads the public 10-minute wind measurement files published as Swiss
Open Government Data at `data.geo.admin.ch`, operated by the Swiss federal
administration. The app downloads the **nationwide** measurement file and
selects the relevant stations locally — it does **not** send your position,
your identity, or any information about you in the request. As with any request
on the internet, your device's IP address is technically visible to that server;
its handling is governed by the Swiss federal administration's own privacy
notice. Wind data is cached on your device so the app keeps working out on the
water without a signal.

Helmer contains no other network code. It has no backend, and I operate no
server that Helmer talks to.

## Maps

The chart is rendered with Apple's MapKit framework. Map tiles, search and
points of interest are provided by Apple Maps and handled under
[Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). I receive no
data from this.

## Notifications

The anchor-drift alarm uses **local** notifications only, including Apple's
critical alerts so the alarm can break through Silent mode and Do Not Disturb.
There is no push server and no device token — notifications are scheduled by the
app on your device.

## App Store privacy label

Helmer is declared as **"Data Not Collected"** on the App Store, which matches
what is described above.

## Children

Helmer collects no data from anyone, including children, and contains no ads,
no in-app purchases and no user-generated content.

## Your rights

This policy is written to satisfy the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection
(FADP/DSG) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Because no
personal data about you is collected, transmitted or stored by me, there is no
data set on which to exercise access, correction, deletion or portability
rights. You are welcome to contact me at
[support@iperka.com](mailto:support@iperka.com) with any question about this.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version of Helmer changes how data is handled, this page will be
updated and the "last updated" date above will change. Material changes will
also be noted in the App Store release notes.
